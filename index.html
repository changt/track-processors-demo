Background replacement demo

Starting camera…
Runs entirely in your browser; no video leaves your device. Built with @livekit/track-processors. Alpha blends the background in by each model's confidence; Mask blurs the mask and cuts it at 0.5, as for a binary mask. MediaPipe runs on WebGL; the other models need WebGPU. MODNet (Apache-2.0, ZHKKKe/MODNet) and video human matting (Apache-2.0, ModelScope) run on LiteRT.js; PP-HumanSeg (Apache-2.0, PaddleSeg) on ONNX Runtime Web.
