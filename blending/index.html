Both settings run the same model, MediaPipe's selfie segmenter, in @livekit/track-processors. Binary (maskBlending: 'binary'): each pixel is the person or the background, with a feathered edge cut at 0.5. Alpha (maskBlending: 'alpha'): the model's confidence that a pixel is the person is that pixel's opacity, so hair and uncertain edges blend into the background. The alpha range is the alphaRange option: confidence at or below the low knob becomes fully background, at or above the high knob fully the person, with a smooth ramp between. At 0 to 1 the confidence is used as it is. Runs entirely in your browser; no video leaves your device. Background photo by Samantha Gades on Unsplash.
