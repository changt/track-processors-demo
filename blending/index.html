Both settings run the same model, MediaPipe's selfie segmenter. Binary is the default of @livekit/track-processors: each pixel is the person or the background, with a feathered edge cut at 0.5. Alpha is the maskBlending: 'alpha' option: the model's confidence that a pixel is the person is that pixel's opacity, so hair and uncertain edges blend into the background. Runs entirely in your browser; no video leaves your device. Background photo by Samantha Gades on Unsplash.
